# <b>OpenTelemetry</b>

### 👨‍🏫 <b>What is OpenTelemetry?</b>

OpenTelemetry is a set of tools and libraries that help <b>collect, process, and export telemetry data</b> (like logs, metrics, and traces) from your app so you can understand what it’s doing and how well it’s performing.

Think of it as the <b>“sensors” inside your code</b> that tell you:

- How slow or fast things are
- What errors are happening
- What dependencies are called
- How much memory/CPU/connections are being used

---

### 🧠 Why do we use OpenTelemetry?

- To <b>observe</b> how requests flow through your app (called <i>tracing</i>)
- To <b>monitor</b> things like DB connections, memory, CPU (called <i>metrics</i>)
- To <b>log</b> important events or errors (<i>logging</i>)

OTel helps you <b>debug production issues</b>, <b>measure performance</b>, and <b>optimize system health</b>.
